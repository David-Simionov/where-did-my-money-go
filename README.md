# 💰 Where Did My Money Go?

**Where Did My Money Go?** is a simple personal finance web app designed for people who often wonder where their money went by the end of the month.

### 🎯 Who is it for?

* Students
* Young adults
* People who want better control over their spending
* Anyone who struggles to track where their money goes

### 💡 What problem does it solve?

Many people spend money throughout the month without realizing how much they are actually spending or where their money goes.

**Where Did My Money Go?** makes it easier to track expenses, understand spending habits, and get a clearer picture of your finances.
